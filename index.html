HI LOVE,

Sorry for upsetting you kanina. Sorry for the misunderstanding between us. I never wanted things to go that way and it really hurts me knowing I made you feel bad. Seeing you upset because of me is one of the worst feelings.

I'm sorry for treating you in ways you don't deserve. You deserve softness, patience, and understanding every single day. Sorry if minsan hindi ko magawa yung treatment na gusto mo or kailangan mo. Alam ko nakakapagod at nakakadismaya yun for you.

Sorry kung lagi na lang ako ganito... puro na lang sorry, parang walang pagbabago. Ayoko na nung feeling na paulit-ulit akong nagsosorry pero wala namang concrete change. You deserve more than empty words. You deserve real effort.

That's why I'm really trying. Trying to be more careful with my words. Trying to think before I react. Trying to be more present and more patient with you. I'm trying to change for real — not just for today, but for the long run. Because I don't want to keep hurting the person I love the most in this world.

Every time I mess up, it weighs so heavy on me. I keep replaying the moment and wishing I handled it better. I never want to be the reason you feel unloved, unheard, or taken for granted. You mean everything to me and I hate that my actions sometimes make you question that.

It's okay love if ayaw mo akong kausapin ngayon. I understand. Hintayin na lang kita hanggang ready ka na ulit para kausapin ako. Hindi ako magmamadali. Gusto ko lang malaman mo na nandito lang ako, waiting, and I won't force you.

Kahit gaano katagal, okay lang. Take your time. Kapag ready ka na, nandito pa rin ako. Hindi ako mawawala. Hindi ako susuko. Gusto ko lang ng chance na ayusin ang lahat ng to and to make things right between us.

Thank you for still staying even when it's hard. Thank you for your patience when I'm difficult. Thank you for loving me through my flaws, my moods, and the days when I'm not at my best. Not everyone would stay... but you do. And that means more to me than you'll ever know.

Always remember this in your heart: I love you so much. More than I can properly put into words. Losing you is the last thing I ever want to experience. That's why I'm still here, still choosing you, still fighting for us even on the hard days.

I'm sorry, love. Truly and deeply. Please don't give up on me. Please don't leave. Kapag ready ka na, let's talk, let's clear everything, and let's get through this together the way we always do.

I promise I'll keep trying to be better for you. For us. Because you are worth every effort. Worth every wait. Worth everything.

I LOVE YOU SO MUCHH
